# 0.1.0-alpha.6

Sixth alpha. Screen recording, temporary upload links, window capture and gallery filters.

## Recording

- `kiekje record`, Record screen in the tray menu or the capture menu starts a recording. The desktop's own picker chooses a screen or window. Run it again (Stop recording) to save a WebM file to `~/Videos/Kiekje`, with a notification to open it or show it in its folder.
- Recording needs the GStreamer PipeWire plugin and the good plugins (`gst-plugin-pipewire` and `gst-plugins-good` on Arch). Without them the recording fails with a notification naming the package, and `kiekje doctor` shows the same.

## Sharing

- Settings > General > Sharing turns on temporary upload (off by default). In the gallery, Upload and copy link (Ctrl+U) sends a capture to Litterbox and copies the link. It expires after 1, 12, 24 or 72 hours.

## Capture

- Window capture is its own mode: `kiekje capture window`, a tray item and a shortcut. Click or press Enter on the hovered window. It works on Plasma 6 and Hyprland; elsewhere it is disabled with the reason.
- Unplugging a monitor during a selection cancels the capture with one notification.

## Gallery

- Filters for kind, period, dimensions, file size, annotated and unsaved.
- With saving off, captures are copied and kept for 7 days under an Unsaved badge, with a Keep action.

## Editor

- Digits pick a colour. Ctrl+scroll changes the size, Shift+scroll the opacity and Alt+scroll the colour.

## General

- An opt-in update check (off by default) and a Check now button in About.
- Settings and `kiekje doctor` say whether a clipboard manager keeps copies after Quit.

## Tested

The recording pipeline was tested in a headless lab against a PipeWire source; the portal picker could not run there. The live upload is untested against Litterbox. CI starts the binary and installs the packages on Arch, Fedora, openSUSE Tumbleweed, Debian trixie, Ubuntu 24.04 and Ubuntu 25.10, headless. Everything in this release is unverified on a real desktop (KDE Plasma, Hyprland, GNOME).

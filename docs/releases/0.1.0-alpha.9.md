# 0.1.0-alpha.9

Ninth alpha. A redesign of every window, a light theme, GIF export for recordings and recordings in the gallery.

## Look

- Every window, the tray popover, the overlay, the editor and the gallery follow one "Crisp tool" design: warm graphite surfaces, one orange accent, Geist in sentence case, 1 px borders and 6 to 10 px radii. The details are on the design page.
- A light theme with warm paper surfaces. `[general] theme = "light"` or the Light theme switch on the General settings page applies it to every window at once.
- Hover, focus, selection and checked states change a background, an icon fill or a text colour. They never add an outline or recolour a border.
- New monochrome symbolic icons for the tray popover, the settings sidebar and the gallery.
- The tray popover is a command palette: a search field, Capture and Record sections, an icon and keycaps on each row.

## Recording

- A finished recording appears in the gallery with a poster frame.
- Export as GIF (Ctrl+G) on a recording, or Export GIF on the Recording saved notification, writes a looping GIF next to the recording at 10 frames per second, at most 800 px wide and cut after 30 seconds.

## Diagnostics

- Log lines read `kiekje: [module] message` and are filtered by `KIEKJE_LOG=error|warn|info|debug` (default `info`). `KIEKJE_LOG=debug kiekje daemon` adds detail when reporting a bug.

## Internal

- The application code moved out of one large module into focused modules for startup, shortcuts, the tray, panels, the capture flow, delivery, saving and recording.
- Errors the app branches on are typed enums per module.
- Release builds use thin LTO and strip symbols.
- `rustls-webpki` and `webpki-roots` are updated for RUSTSEC-2026-0381 and RUSTSEC-2026-0382, which affect the update check and uploads.
- Capturing across monitors grabs only the monitors a selection touches.
- A dependency audit workflow runs `cargo deny`.

## Tested

`cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy -D warnings` and the unit tests pass on the release commit (507 tests in the app, 11 in the lab probe). The redesign, the light theme, GIF export and the gallery poster frames have not been run on a real desktop yet and are unverified. Hyprland and GNOME are untested. CI starts the binary and installs the packages on Arch, Fedora, openSUSE Tumbleweed, Debian trixie, Ubuntu 24.04 and Ubuntu 25.10, headless.

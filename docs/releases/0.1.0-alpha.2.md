Second alpha, and the first with a downloadable build.

## Drawing

- Thinner lines by default: 3 px on a 1080p capture, scaled up to 2x on HiDPI.
- Size (1 to 24 px) and Opacity (10 to 100%) sliders replace Thin, Regular and Bold.
- Colour, size and opacity are remembered between editors.
- Changing a slider restyles the selected shape; a whole drag is one undo step.
- Right-click the canvas for a menu with every tool, line size, undo, redo and delete.
- Every tool and thinner/thicker lines have a key, changeable on Settings, Shortcuts, Drawing keys.

## Packaging

- Releases ship an x86_64 Linux build with its sha256.

## Tested

Alpha.1 was tested on KDE Plasma (Wayland) only. The new drawing features are unverified on a real desktop; Hyprland and GNOME are unverified.

# 0.1.0-alpha.13

Thirteenth alpha. Capture and record buttons slide out of a screen corner.

## Edge dock

- Push the pointer into the chosen corner of a screen and a column of five buttons slides out: Capture every screen, Capture region, Record screen, Record a GIF of a region and Copy text from region. While a recording runs, Record screen becomes Stop recording.
- Hovering a button shows its name and the shortcut bound to it. A button that can't be used, such as Copy text without Tesseract, says why.
- A click hides the dock before the capture starts, so it never shows up in the frame. The dock slides back 350 ms after the pointer leaves.
- Turn it on and pick top left, top right, bottom left or bottom right in Settings, General, Edge dock. It is stored as `[dock] enabled` (off by default) and `[dock] corner` (default `"top-right"`).
- Each screen gets its own dock, except on an edge shared with another screen, so moving between screens doesn't open it.
- It needs layer shell: KDE Plasma, Hyprland or Sway. Elsewhere the setting is disabled with the reason.

## Tested

`cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy -D warnings` and the unit tests pass (568 tests in the app, 11 in the lab probe). In a nested KWin the dock and its tooltips showed in the top-left and bottom-right corners. On KDE Plasma 6 the dock's surface mapped in its corner and opened when the pointer entered it. Clicking each button end to end on a real desktop is unverified. Hyprland, Sway and GNOME are untested.

# 0.1.0-alpha.8

Eighth alpha. Emoji search in English and Dutch, turning and multi-selection in the editor, and a documentation site.

## Editor

- The emoji picker searches every Unicode emoji by its English and Dutch names and keywords from Unicode CLDR and tolerates a typo, so `vuur`, `insekt` and `rockett` all find something. Chat emoticons such as `:)`, `:p`, `xD`, `<3` and `(y)` find their emoji, and `:fire:` works too.
- A Recent row keeps the last eight stamped emoji in the library.
- The picker works from the keyboard alone: type from anywhere, Down into the rows, arrows between emoji, Up back to the field, Enter or Space to use one, Escape to close. A line under the grid names the emoji under the pointer or focus. Pressing `:` in the editor opens the picker with the colon typed.
- Text, checks, crosses and emoji can be turned. Drag the round handle above a selected one, or drag just outside a corner where a curved arrow shows. Near a right angle it snaps, and Shift keeps 15 degree steps. `,` and `.` turn in 15 degree steps (`turn_left` and `turn_right` under `[drawing.keys]`).
- Several shapes can be selected at once: drag a frame on empty canvas with Select, Ctrl+click, or Ctrl+A. Move, nudge, turn, restyle and delete apply to all of them and undo as one step. A group has its own frame and turn handle and swings around its centre.
- A placed stamp is selected right away and the tool returns to Select, so the faded preview stops following the pointer.
- The editor window keeps the scaled picture between frames and clips faded shapes to their size. Dragging and the stamp preview no longer stutter on large screens.

## Gallery

- Search tolerates a typo in the file name and ignores accents.

## Settings

- Ctrl+plus, Ctrl+minus and Ctrl+0 zoom the settings window.

## Documentation

- The documentation is a site built from `docs/` and published to GitHub Pages, with a guide, a reference and the release notes.

## Tested

The release build ran on KDE Plasma 6 on Wayland at 3840x2160, scale 1. The emoji search, emoticons, the Recent row, turning one shape and multi-selection were tried there and fixed along the way. The final versions of group turning, frame selection and the drawing speed fix are unverified on a desktop. The speed fix was measured in a headless render of a 2560x1440 picture: painting it under the shapes at zoom 0.75 went from a mean of 45 ms to 0.8 ms per frame over 60 frames. Hyprland and GNOME are untested. CI starts the binary and installs the packages on Arch, Fedora, openSUSE Tumbleweed, Debian trixie, Ubuntu 24.04 and Ubuntu 25.10, headless.

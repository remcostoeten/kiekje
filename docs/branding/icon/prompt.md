# Kiekje app icon prompt

Paste the prompt below into ChatGPT and attach every image from `references/`.

## Prompt

```
I'm attaching 8 reference app icons. Study what they share: an iOS squircle, one bold
simple silhouette in the center, soft 3D volume with smooth gradients, subtle glow or
frosted glass, a calm background and no text. Match that level of craft. Do not copy
any of them.

About the app:
Kiekje (Dutch for "snapshot", a casual, affectionate word for a quick photo) is a fast,
native screenshot app for Linux desktops. It lives in the system tray. When you press the
shortcut it instantly freezes the screen, lets you drag a selection over that frozen
frame, then copies the result to the clipboard, saves it and keeps it in a small gallery.
An annotation editor lets you add arrows, boxes, text and blur. It feels like a polished
macOS utility (think CleanShot X) but is built for Linux: quick, quiet, out of your way,
a little playful. The UI is dark-only, minimal, with a single vivid orange accent
(#fe5101), Geist typography and white crop-style corner marks around selections.

Brand feel for the icon: instant, precise, friendly, crafted. A tiny helper that grabs
the moment. Not corporate, not a camera clone.

Design a premium iOS-quality app icon for Kiekje.

Shape: a perfect iOS squircle with continuous corners. Transparent background outside
the squircle.

Icon background: deep near-black charcoal (#141414) with a very subtle radial lift toward
the top and a faint warm orange glow behind the subject.

Subject: one bold, simple, friendly symbol filling about 60% of the icon. A soft, glossy,
rounded blob character (a cute little "snapshot" creature) with two small oval eyes,
made of a smooth matte-gloss material with a warm gradient from #fe5101 to soft peach
and a hint of pink at the top edge. Gentle subsurface glow, soft specular highlight at
the top left, subtle inner shadow at the bottom, small soft contact shadow underneath.

Around the subject: four thick rounded screenshot selection corner brackets in clean
white, like a crop frame, slightly overlapping the character's edges so it feels like
it is being captured.

Style: minimal, clean vector-like 3D, soft studio lighting, crisp edges, a high-contrast
silhouette that still reads at 32px. No text, no letters, no camera, no extra objects,
no noise, no busy details.

Output: 1024x1024 PNG, centered, straight-on front view.
```

## Variants

Swap the "Subject" paragraph for one of these to explore other directions.

- **Shutter:** a chunky soft 3D camera aperture made of six rounded #fe5101 blades, slightly open, with a frosted glass center glowing warm white.
- **Sticker** (like references 01 and 07): a flat white sticker-outlined blob with a thick white border and a black inner shape, framed by white crop corners, on a solid #fe5101 squircle.
- **Frosted glass** (like references 05 and 06): a frosted translucent rounded square, like a captured fragment of a screen, with an orange-to-violet gradient blurred inside and a thin bright rim light.

## Follow-up prompts

- "Give me 4 variations of this in one grid so I can compare them."
- "Keep everything the same but make the silhouette simpler so it reads at 16px."
- "Make a flat monochrome version of the same symbol for a system tray icon, white on transparent, no gradients."

## Checks before picking one

- View it at 32px and 16px. If the corner brackets merge into mush, drop them for small sizes.
- The colour version is for the app and launcher icon only. Icons inside the UI stay monochrome and symbolic.
